About Kingdom Kings Ijiebor

Kingdom Kings Ijiebor is a Christian minister, Bible teacher, theology lecturer, leadership coach, entrepreneur, and educator committed to helping people discover truth, build wisdom, and fulfill purpose.

Personal Ministerial Statement

His ministry is built on the conviction that transformation begins with truth, wisdom, character, and intentional development. He teaches that growth should shape character, relationships, leadership, work, decision-making, and contribution to society.

Credentials

He is a holder of an International Ministerial License and Ministerial Certificate from International Bible Academy USA. He also brings a background in Computer Science and practical experience in digital technology and web development.

Areas of Ministry & Expertise

Biblical Teaching

Helping people understand and apply God’s Word to everyday life with clarity and purpose.

Leadership Development

Equipping leaders with principles of responsibility, discipline, vision, and wisdom.

Personal Growth

Supporting individuals in confidence, mindset, character, and meaningful progress.

“My ministry is not simply about informing people; it is about helping people become.”
